Weight & real burn

needs data
Import from a Renpho scale
Export your measurements from the Renpho Health app as a CSV (it usually arrives by email), save the attachment, then choose it here. Any CSV with a date column and a weight column works, in kg, lb or stone. If Renpho is set to sync with Apple Health, the Apple Health import on the Sources tab now brings weight in too.
A screenshot of the Renpho history or trend list works too. This uses Claude.

Which burn counts

Wearables disagree, often by several hundred kcal. Pick one and judge your trend against it rather than switching week to week.

Garmin

no data
Garmin Connect shows both under Calories (Resting + Active = Total).
Import Garmin Connect CSV
Garmin Connect (web) → Reports → Health & Fitness → Calories → Export CSV. Any CSV with a date column and active/total calorie columns will do; headers are matched by name.

Apple Watch

no data
Fitness app → Activity ring shows Active; Health → Resting Energy shows the rest. Leave Resting blank to use your BMR.
Import Health export or Shortcuts CSV
Health app → profile → Export All Health Data → unzip → choose export.xml (it can be hundreds of MB; it is read in chunks). Or a CSV from a Shortcut with date, active and resting columns.
Paste from an iPhone Shortcut
Build a Shortcut once: Find Health Samples for Active Energy (today) and take the sum; the same for Resting Energy; Find the latest Weight sample; then a Text action reading today active=SUM resting=SUM kg=WEIGHT with those values dropped in, and Copy to Clipboard. Run it, then paste here. Several lines with dates like 2026-11-10 active=480 work too.

Import from Cronometer

On the Cronometer website: Settings, Account, Account Data, Export Data. Pick a date range (a few months is plenty) and choose Export Servings. Then select that file here. Every food you logged is added to your foods, most-eaten first.

Screenshots of your Cronometer diary work too. Stoke reads each food with its amount and calories and saves it to your foods; tick the box above to log them on the day shown as well. This uses Claude.
